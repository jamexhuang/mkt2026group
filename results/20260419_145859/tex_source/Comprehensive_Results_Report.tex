\section*{Executive Summary / 執行摘要}

This report presents the complete statistical analysis results for the Booking.com social media engagement study (v3.0.0). Compared to v2 (N=766), this version applies language detection to exclude non-English posts, yielding a cleaner sample of N=624 English-language posts. All analyses use Python v3.0.0 statistical scripts with robust standard errors (HC1). 本報告呈現 Booking.com 社群媒體互動研究的完整統計分析結果（v3.0.0）。相較於 v2（N=766），本版本使用語言偵測排除非英文貼文，最終分析樣本為 N=624 篇英文貼文。所有分析均使用 Python v3.0.0 統計腳本執行，並採用穩健標準誤（HC1）。語言過濾提升了英文詞典（情感效價、真誠性）的適用性，並獲得稍高的解釋力（$R^2 = 0.501$）。

\section{Research Framework Verification / 研究架構驗證}

\subsection{Research Question / 研究問題}
\textbf{How do textual features in Booking.com's posts on X influence social media engagement?}

Booking.com 在 X 上的貼文文字特徵，如何影響社群互動表現？

\subsection{Hypotheses / 研究假說}

The study tests five main hypotheses:

\begin{enumerate}[label=H\arabic*.]
    \item Text length is positively associated with social media engagement.
    \item Question marks are positively associated with social media engagement.
    \item Emotional valence is positively associated with social media engagement.
    \item The number of hashtags is positively associated with social media engagement.
    \item Emoji usage is positively associated with social media engagement.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Model Specification / 模型規格}

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\log(1 + \text{Engagement}_i) = {}& \beta_0 + \beta_1\text{TextLength}_i + \beta_2\text{Question}_i + \beta_3\text{Valence}_i \\
& + \beta_4\text{Hashtag}_i + \beta_5\text{Emoji}_i + \beta_6\text{Picture}_i + \beta_7\text{URL}_i \\
& + \beta_8\text{AtMention}_i + \varepsilon_i
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\text{Engagement}_i = \text{like}_i + \text{comment}_i + \text{share}_i$
    \item The dependent variable is log-transformed as $\log(1 + \text{Engagement}_i)$ to address right-skewness
    \item Independent variables: TextLength (H1), Question (H2), Valence (H3), Hashtag (H4), Emoji (H5)
    \item Control variables: Picture, URL, AtMention
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Description / 資料說明}

\subsection{Dataset Characteristics / 資料集特徵}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Original Sample:} 766 posts from Booking.com's official X (Twitter) account
    \item \textbf{Language Filter:} 139 non-English posts removed (Spanish, French, Portuguese, and others)
    \item \textbf{Zero-length Filter:} 3 additional posts removed (no usable English text content)
    \item \textbf{Analysis Sample:} N = 624 English-language posts
    \item \textbf{Time Period:} November 1, 2021 to November 21, 2022
    \item \textbf{Data Source:} \texttt{data/research\_data.csv}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Preprocessing / 資料前處理}

Non-English posts were identified using the \texttt{langdetect} library applied to tweet text after removing URLs, @mentions, and hashtags. Posts detected as non-English (Spanish, French, Portuguese, and others) were excluded from all analyses, as the English-language sentiment and brand personality lexicons used in this study are not applicable to non-English content. Posts with zero English words after text cleaning were also excluded. The final analysis sample comprises N = 624 English-language marketing and customer engagement posts.

非英文貼文使用 \texttt{langdetect} 套件進行偵測，偵測前先移除 URLs、@提及與 Hashtag。偵測為非英文（西班牙文、法文、葡萄牙文等）的貼文均排除於所有分析之外，因為本研究使用的英文情感詞典與品牌個性詞典不適用於非英文文本。文字清理後無英文詞彙的貼文亦被排除。最終分析樣本為 624 篇英文行銷與顧客互動貼文。

\subsection{Interpretation of Coefficients / 係數解釋}

\subsubsection{Text Length (H1) - REJECTED}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Coefficient:} $\beta_1 = -0.036$ ($p < .001$)
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} Each additional word in a post is associated with a 3.6\% decrease in log-transformed engagement, holding other factors constant. This effect is stronger than in v2 ($-0.021$), as non-English posts with truncated length inflate the true effect.
    \item \textbf{Implication:} Brevity is even more strongly rewarded in English-language content on X.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Question Mark (H2) - SUPPORTED}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Coefficient:} $\beta_2 = 0.305$ ($p = .043$)
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} Posts containing a question mark have approximately 35.6\% higher engagement ($e^{0.305} - 1 \approx 0.356$) compared to posts without questions.
    \item \textbf{Implication:} Interactive cues like questions significantly boost user participation, though the effect is somewhat attenuated versus v2 ($0.479$).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Emotional Valence (H3) - SUPPORTED}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Coefficient:} $\beta_3 = 0.065$ ($p = .030$)
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} A one-unit increase in emotional valence score is associated with a 6.7\% increase in engagement ($e^{0.065} - 1 \approx 0.067$).
    \item \textbf{Implication:} Positive emotional tone resonates with audiences in the travel context.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Hashtag Count (H4) - SUPPORTED}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Coefficient:} $\beta_4 = 0.904$ ($p < .001$)
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} Each additional hashtag is associated with approximately 147\% higher engagement ($e^{0.904} - 1 \approx 1.47$).
    \item \textbf{Implication:} Hashtags dramatically improve post discoverability and engagement.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Emoji Count (H5) - SUPPORTED}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Coefficient:} $\beta_5 = 0.286$ ($p = .001$)
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} Each additional emoji is associated with approximately 33.1\% higher engagement ($e^{0.286} - 1 \approx 0.331$).
    \item \textbf{Implication:} Visual-linguistic cues enhance post expressiveness and attract user attention.
\end{itemize}

\section{Managerial Implications / 管理意涵}

\subsection{Key Recommendations for Booking.com / 給 Booking.com 的關鍵建議}

\subsubsection{1. Embrace Brevity Even More / 更加擁抱簡潔}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Finding:} Text length negatively impacts engagement ($\beta = -0.036, p < .001$) — a stronger effect than v2
    \item \textbf{Recommendation:} Keep English posts concise (under 20 words when possible).
    \item \textbf{Action:} Establish word count guidelines for English-language social media copywriters.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{2. Use Questions Strategically / 策略性使用提問}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Finding:} Question marks boost engagement by 35.6\% ($\beta = 0.305, p = .043$)
    \item \textbf{Recommendation:} Incorporate questions in English posts to encourage user interaction.
    \item \textbf{Example:} "Where's your dream destination? 🌍" instead of "Book your dream destination."
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{3. Maintain Positive Emotional Tone / 維持正向情緒語調}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Finding:} Positive valence increases engagement by 6.7\% per unit ($\beta = 0.065, p = .030$)
    \item \textbf{Recommendation:} Use aspirational, exciting, and positive language in English content.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{4. Maximize Hashtag Usage / 最大化 Hashtag 使用}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Finding:} Each hashtag more than doubles engagement ($\beta = 0.904, p < .001$)
    \item \textbf{Recommendation:} Include 1-2 relevant hashtags per English post to enhance discoverability.
    \item \textbf{Strategy:} Use trending travel hashtags (e.g., \#TravelTuesday, \#Wanderlust) and destination-specific tags.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{5. Leverage Emoji Strategically / 策略性運用 Emoji}
\begin{itemize}

\section{Limitations and Future Research / 限制與未來研究}

\subsection{Study Limitations / 研究限制}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Language Filter Constraint:} Restricting to English-only posts (N=624 from 766) limits generalizability to Booking.com's multilingual communication strategy. Non-English posts may follow different engagement patterns.

    \item \textbf{Cross-Sectional Design:} Our data captures posts over one year but does not establish causal relationships.

    \item \textbf{Platform-Specific:} Results are specific to X (Twitter). Engagement patterns may differ on other platforms.

    \item \textbf{Single Brand:} The study focuses exclusively on Booking.com.

    \item \textbf{Engagement Aggregation:} The dependent variable combines likes, comments, and shares equally.

    \item \textbf{Time Period:} Data from November 2021 to November 2022 may not reflect current social media trends in 2026.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Future Research Directions / 未來研究方向}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Multilingual Analysis:} Develop language-specific models for Spanish and French posts separately.
    \item \textbf{Disaggregated Engagement:} Examine likes, comments, and shares as separate dependent variables.
    \item \textbf{Temporal Dynamics:} Investigate how posting time and seasonal factors moderate textual feature effects.
    \item \textbf{Content Interactions:} Test interaction effects between textual features.
    \item \textbf{Longitudinal Studies:} Track changes in engagement drivers over multiple years.
\end{enumerate}
